60-Second Executive Takeaways
  • 2026 operations are tracking near breakeven: Through September, operating spend finished just $17.5k above operating revenue—even with a +$180k YTD spike in Repairs (plumbing, leaks, fire) and higher heating oil costs—because the 9% common charge increase enacted in January and payroll savings absorbed the pressure (2026 full-year est.: -$13.4k).

  • Balance sheet stays rock-solid: Core rainy-day reserves stand at $1,355,062 (100% untouched), total cash is $1.90M, debt is $0, and arrears across 249 units are just $7,616.
